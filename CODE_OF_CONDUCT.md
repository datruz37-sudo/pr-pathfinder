# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of background, identity, experience level, or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable feedback, and focus disagreement on ideas
rather than people. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and sustained disruption are
not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban
participants whose behavior is harmful. Incidents should be reported privately through the
security advisory feature of the GitHub repository until a dedicated conduct address is listed.
Reports will be reviewed confidentially and as promptly as practical.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
